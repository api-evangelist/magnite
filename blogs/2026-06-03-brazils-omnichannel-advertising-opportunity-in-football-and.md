As sports viewing shifts toward streaming, the 2026 football tournament will showcase how omnichannel advertising combines live TV, streaming platforms, and mobile engagement. Publishers and advertisers in Brazil are leveraging first-party data and programmatic technology to create integrated ecosystems that deliver synchronized storytelling across multiple screens, with second-screen behavior amplifying rather than fragmenting viewer attention.
