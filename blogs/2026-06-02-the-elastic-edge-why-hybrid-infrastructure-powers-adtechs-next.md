Magnite examines how hybrid infrastructure combining owned data centers with cloud resources enables advertising technology platforms to handle unpredictable demand spikes from live streaming events while maintaining cost efficiency. The company argues that this integrated approach outperforms purely on-premise or cloud-only models by providing elasticity, partnership flexibility, and support for emerging AI and containerized workloads.
